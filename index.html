How it works

  1. 1

    The desk publishes

    An authorised operator creates a signal, reviews it, and confirms. The system checks the levels make sense before anything goes out.

  2. 2

    You're alerted

    The signal reaches the app with its entry, stop and targets. You decide whether and how to trade it, sized to your own risk.

  3. 3

    The outcome is recorded

    Targets, stop moves and the close are added to the timeline. The result goes into the permanent ledger, win or lose.

Losses can't be hidden

Most signal services ask you to trust a screenshot. GBL is built so the record doesn't depend on trust.

  • Published levels can't be changed, even by us. The database refuses the edit.
  • Every update (stop moved, target hit, close) is a separate event with who, when and why.
  • The trade ledger is append-only. Rows are added when a signal closes and never removed.
  • Every important action is written to an audit log that can't be rewritten.

Our live record opens at launch

We won't show a win rate until there is real data behind it. When trading starts, the full ledger goes in the app and on this page: every trade, with entry, exit, result and timestamps.

Results are shown in R. 1R is the amount risked on the trade. Hypothetical or simulated results will always be labelled as such.

Questions

Is this financial advice?

No. Signals are trade ideas from the GBL desk. They are not personal advice, and you are responsible for your own decisions and position size.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Futures are leveraged and losses can exceed what you put in. Every GBL signal has a stop, but stops can slip in fast markets. Only trade money you can afford to lose.

What is R?

R is a unit of risk. If a trade risks 20 points and exits 40 points in profit, it made +2R. Using R means results are comparable whatever your account size.

Which markets do you cover?

CME index futures: NQ (Nasdaq-100), ES (S&P 500), YM (Dow) and RTY (Russell 2000).

How do I know results aren't edited?

Published levels are locked at the database level, updates are separate timestamped events, and the ledger only ever grows. You can see the whole history in the app.

Is automation available?

Not yet. It is planned, with paper trading first and strict risk limits. It is not included in the current plan.

Is it available in my country?

Not everywhere yet. The app checks your country when you sign up. For now it is not open to people in the United States.
